# Travel checklist

Build a checklist only from the trip details supplied by the user.

1. Extract dates, duration, transport mode, activities, constraints, and stated
   weather assumptions.
2. Group items into documents, clothing, equipment, reservations to verify, and
   departure-day checks.
3. Mark any item derived from an assumption.
4. End with a short list of missing details that could materially change the
   checklist.

Do not make bookings, purchases, messages, or live availability claims. If the
user needs current weather, schedules, entry rules, or prices, state that those
facts require a separately authorized and current source.
